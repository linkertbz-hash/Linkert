# Finansteh — Financial information platform

**Public website:** https://finansteh.ru  
**Role:** Product owner; planning, iteration, product QA and AI-assisted implementation.

## Challenge

Visitors researching financial products need understandable content, structured navigation and convenient ways to find relevant information.

## Solution (publicly visible)

- Financial topic and product-category pages.
- Editorial content supporting information discovery.
- UX improvements to navigation, readability and responsive presentation.
- Workflows for keeping the content current.
- Ongoing attention to SEO and measurement of user interaction.

## Product considerations

Emphasis on clarity, consistent user journeys, content quality and gradual product improvement based on actual behavior and reliable analytics.

## What to see

Review the public journal, explore categories and inspect article presentation on mobile and desktop.

## Result and next steps

A live financial information platform under active improvement. Future assessment focuses on verified search visibility, content quality and usability outcomes.
